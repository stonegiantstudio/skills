Same method as `human-writing/tests/results-2026-10-20.md`: Claude Sonnet 5.5
through the API, five drafts per arm, graded blind. Arms:

- **control** — no skill;
- **1.5** — the skill as it stood before this release;
- **v1** — the first round of edits;
- **v2** — the shipped text.

Each table counts the drafts that failed each check, out of five.

The one v2 failure is an invented motive: "we made it for practice between
classes," where the sheet says only that learners use it between classes.
Round 3's edits touched only human-writing's catalogue, which this skill loads
for its sweep, so one failure in five is within run-to-run variation.

## What each edit answers

- **Claims about other people need a source.** Every 1.5 draft followed the
  brief and wrote that schools "expect you to arrive already knowing the
  vocabulary." Nothing on the sheet says so. Every v1 and v2 draft built the
  positioning from the product instead and said in its notes why.
- **A real reason to act now.** Three 1.5 drafts invented urgency the sheet
  didn't support: "Your next class is coming either way," "Every day you wait
  is a day of reviews you don't get." The reference files had taught "Only 3
  spots left" with no condition; they now tie urgency to a real deadline.
- **Provenance list and real numbers.** The "11,847 customers" example now
  asks for the real count from your records.

## Unchanged at baseline

Checks 3 and 5 never failed, so this release adds nothing for them. The
"delete, don't disclaim" test the plan proposed is left out for that
reason.
